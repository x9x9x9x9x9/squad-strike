Squad Strike for melee-pc - getting a Windows build
=====================================================

These files are a change to melee-pc (github.com/999sian/melee-pc), made
against commit 0fb0af3 (v0.4.2, 21 Oct 2026). GitHub builds it for free.

1. Sign in to github.com and open https://github.com/999sian/melee-pc
   Click "Fork", then "Create fork".
2. In your fork, open the "Actions" tab and click the green button to
   enable workflows.
3. In your fork's main page: "Add file" -> "Upload files".
   Drag in the CONTENTS of this folder (src, cmake, docs and README.md;
   not this .txt file). GitHub keeps the folder paths and replaces the
   existing files.
   At the bottom choose "Create a new branch", name it squad-strike,
   and click "Propose changes" (you don't need to open a pull request).
4. Open "Actions" again. A "Build" run starts for the squad-strike branch.
   When the "Windows (x86_64)" job is green (about 20-30 minutes), open the
   run, scroll to "Artifacts" and download "windows-x86_64".
   It contains Melee-Windows-x86_64.zip: unzip it and run melee.exe with
   your own GALE01 disc image, as with any melee-pc build.
   (Other jobs in the run, like macOS or iOS, may fail; you don't need them.)
5. For online play, send your friend that same zip. Netplay only connects
   two copies of the same build.

Playing: see docs/squad-strike.md. Squads work offline and in
VS Mode -> ONLINE -> LAN PLAY or DIRECT CONNECT (not Unranked/Ranked).

If upstream melee-pc has moved on since 0fb0af3 and the build fails, the
uploaded files may be older than your fork's: send the error log and they
can be brought up to date (squad-strike.patch has the exact change).
